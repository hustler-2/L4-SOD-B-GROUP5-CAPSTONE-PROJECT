🎫 Reserve Now, Pay at the Stage
Your seats will be reserved for 30 minutes from now. Present your booking code to the ticket booth or driver before boarding.
  1. Get your booking code instantly after confirming
  2. Arrive at the stage 10 minutes before departure
  3. Show your code at the ticket booth or to the driver
  4. Pay in cash and board your bus
⚠️ Unredeemed reservations are released 10 min before departure.
